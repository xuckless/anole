Video call benchmark

1:1 calls on one CPU and 2 GB (no swap). Each user streams 1080p H.264 at 4 Mbit/s and Opus audio to its peer, and chats every 3 s; the server deflates every packet it relays. One user joins every step until the server crashes or calls collapse. Drag the slider to compare the four servers at any number of concurrent users.

Summary

Good quality: at least 99% of video frames shown and 99% of audio packets played on time, with video p95 one-way latency at most 200 ms. "Shown" means arrived within 200 ms and every frame since its keyframe did too. VMAF and PSNR are for the probe call (users 0 and 1), rebuilt frame by frame from what arrived.

CPU: the container is charged for its own work and its system calls, but not for the kernel's network interrupt work on its core. At saturation that is 10–25% of the core, so a full core shows as 0.73–0.88 here. Users are added 10 at a time every 5 s.
